# First Tech Federal Credit Union

> First Tech Federal Credit Union (First Tech) is a member-owned financial institution specialized in serving the world's leading technology-oriented companies, their employees and families. 

First Tech invests in its communities by supporting the next generation of leaders, thinkers, and innovators. First Tech offers a full range of financial services, including traditional banking, online banking, mortgages, financial planning, various consumer loans, and insurance services. Federally insured by NCUA. Equal Housing Lender.
 
# Products and Services
First Tech FCU provides a full range of consumer financial products and services, including:
- Checking and savings accounts
- Certificates of deposit (CDs) and Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs)
- Home loans and mortgage products
- Auto loans, personal loans, and credit cards
- Insurance products
- Investment and wealth management services
 
These offerings are designed to support members across their full financial lifecycle.
 
# Digital and In-Person Access
Members can manage their finances through multiple service channels, including:
- Online banking
- Mobile banking applications
- In-person service at local branch locations
- Phone-based member support
 
# Value Proposition
First Tech FCU focuses on delivering tailored financial solutions for its members, with an emphasis on:
- Competitive deposit rates
- Discounted lending rates compared to many national and regional banks
- Personalized member service
 
# Community and Employer Partnerships
The credit union maintains partnerships with technology-focused employers and participates in community initiatives. These efforts emphasize member well-being, long-term financial stability, and support for the technology workforce.
 
# Financial Education
First Tech FCU offers educational resources to support financial literacy, including:
- Financial calculators
- Articles and guides on banking, borrowing, saving, and investing
- Tools designed to help members make informed financial decisions
 
# Recognition and Trust
First Tech FCU has received multiple third-party recognitions, including:
- "Most Trusted Brand" and "Best Customer Service" honors (USA Today, 2026)
- High consumer ratings from Trustpilot, WalletHub, and Supermoney
- Industry accolades for its Rewards Checking account from NerdWallet, U.S. News & World Report, and Bankrate
- Strong mobile app ratings on the Apple App Store and Google Play
 
First Tech FCU is recognized for its member-first service model, digital banking experience, and commitment to trust and reliability.
